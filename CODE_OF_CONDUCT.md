# Code of Conduct

OpenDevOps welcomes constructive contributions from people with varied experience levels and backgrounds.

We expect participants to:
- Be respectful, patient, and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Critique ideas and code without personal attacks or harassment.
- Respect privacy and keep credentials, security reports, and private data confidential.
- Provide practical evidence, reproducible steps, and constructive alternatives when disagreeing.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and abusive behavior are not acceptable.

For conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/ch-muhammad-asim). Do not post sensitive allegations or personal details in a public issue. Maintainers may remove inappropriate contributions and limit participation as needed.
